Legal & Privacy Disclosures

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India

1. Information We Collect

ShopSmart collects information to fulfill orders and improve your shopping experience across our digital catalog. This includes:

  • Account Information: Full name, verified email address, and 10-digit Indian mobile contact number when registering.
  • Delivery Details: House/flat number, street, area, city (default Coimbatore), state (Tamil Nadu), and 6-digit PIN code.
  • Shopping Preferences: Cart items, wishlist records, recently viewed catalog products, and product comparisons.
  • Authentication Data: User credentials are secure using Web Crypto SHA-256 cryptographic password hashing. Session and profile states are preserved securely within client LocalStorage.

2. How Your Information is Used

We use the collected information solely for valid e-commerce operations:

  • Order placement, fulfillment, and generation of printable Indian GST-compliant order summaries.
  • Persistent shopping carts and synchronized wishlists across sessions.
  • AI Shopping Assistant and Budget recommendation algorithms operating over our catalog.
  • Customer service communication for delivery updates originating from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

3. Storage and Data Security

ShopSmart does NOT store raw passwords or payment banking credentials (card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs). All demonstration user accounts use industry-standard Web Crypto SHA-256 password hashing. Shopping cart, wishlist, and simulated order records are persisted securely in your browser's LocalStorage.

4. Voice & Camera Permissions

Voice search utilizes the browser's native Web Speech API only upon explicit user consent via microphone permission dialogs. Audio streams are not recorded or persisted to servers. Image-based search previews files locally in the browser to query relevant catalog categories.
